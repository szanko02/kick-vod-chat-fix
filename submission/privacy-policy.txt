Kick VOD Chat Fix privacy policy

The extension has no analytics, advertising, telemetry, remote configuration,
or external reporting service. It does not collect chat messages, usernames,
account credentials, cookies, browsing history, or identifiers for its author.

The only saved setting is whether the fix is enabled. It stays in Firefox's
local extension storage. Playback time, buffer size, request state, and other
numeric diagnostics stay in the browser and are not automatically uploaded.
The user may select and copy the diagnostic report themselves.

The extension modifies the replay loading logic in a recognized public Kick
website script. Kick's own page continues to request chat history from Kick,
using the channel identifier and the replay timestamp, under Kick's own
privacy policy. The extension does not make separate network requests, read
authentication cookies, or send data to its author or another service.

Uninstalling removes the extension's saved setting. There is no server-side
extension data to delete. This extension is independent of Kick and Mozilla.
